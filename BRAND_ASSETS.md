# CHRISÉA Jewelry Brand Assets

## Primary Logo

File: `assets/brand/chrisea-logo-primary.png`

The supplied primary logo uses an ivory-gold illustration and typography on a deep burgundy background. It includes the CHRISÉA wordmark, the word “Jewelry”, a jewellery-bust illustration, decorative stars and the establishment year 2026.

## Website Logo Variants

- `assets/brand/chrisea-logo-full-transparent.png` — transparent full emblem used in the homepage hero and footer
- `assets/brand/chrisea-logo-wordmark.png` — compact horizontal wordmark used in the website navigation

## Usage Guidelines

- Preserve the original aspect ratio.
- Keep the spelling `CHRISÉA`, including the acute accent on `É`.
- Do not recolour, stretch, rotate or add effects to the master image.
- Maintain clear space around the logo so text and product photography do not overlap it.
- Use descriptive alternative text: `CHRISÉA Jewelry logo`.
- Use the supplied image on burgundy or visually compatible sections where the square composition fits.

## Additional Versions Still Recommended

Before launch, request or prepare approved source-quality versions from the brand owner:

- Transparent-background PNG for headers and overlays.
- SVG or other vector master for sharp scaling and print use.
- Horizontal wordmark for desktop navigation.
- Compact icon/monogram for mobile navigation.
- Square favicon and app icons in standard sizes.
- Light and dark one-colour variants.

Any derived logo must retain the approved artwork and should be reviewed by the brand owner before publication.
